TOPOBENCH RESEARCH LICENCE 1.0 — TRANSITION AND REVIEW NOTES
12 October 2026

The canonical licence is the repository's root LICENSE. These notes are
not part of that licence.

EFFECT OF THIS CHANGE

This change replaces the root MIT licence with TopoBench Research Licence
1.0 and aligns package metadata, documentation and contribution guidance.
Merging it into main applies that licence to that checkout; it does not
publish a package or alter an existing release.

The original MIT text and copyright notice are retained in
LICENSES/TopoBench-MIT.txt, with scope identified in NOTICE. The baseline
used for this change is commit 27956913ec7146cded69dd8fb8ebab7b86ca7fcc.
Changing a licence file does not withdraw permissions already validly
granted for earlier material or make unchanged MIT code require payment.
New copyrightable contributions offered only under the research licence
do not acquire MIT permissions from those retained notices.

POLICY

- Eligible noncommercial research, education and reproducibility are free.
- Company use, including internal research, trials and benchmarks, requires
  a separate written commercial agreement from the relevant rights holders
  or their authorised representatives.
- Independent company-funded grants remain eligible. Company-directed or
  company-commissioned work, private company deliverables and exclusive
  sponsor rights are excluded.
- Redistributed covered code and modifications must include corresponding
  source and remain under the same research licence.
- Published or publicly distributed outputs describing work performed
  using the Software must acknowledge TopoBench, identify the version or
  commit and cite the principal publication designated with that version.
  Private work need not be published. Commercial publishers are allowed.
- Missing required publication citations are breaches under clause 9;
  they are not covered by its limited automatic remedy for clause 3's
  notice, source-delivery and licence-delivery failures.
- Research outputs do not inherit the software licence merely because
  TopoBench produced them.

INFRASTRUCTURE DECISION

PENDING: whether to retain clause 5's limited permission for ordinary
hosting, package distribution and rented compute. The current wording
permits those providers to act only on an eligible researcher's
instructions. It grants no permission for providers' own research,
benchmarking, model training or TopoBench-based services.

LICENSING AUTHORITY AND COMMERCIAL ARRANGEMENTS

This change proceeds on the agreed assumption that Lev Telyatnikov and
Guillermo Bernardez Gil have the rights and permissions needed to offer
the research and commercial licences. It does not record an independent
rights audit.

The public licence intentionally contains no named two-person approval
or signature requirement. The desired rule that both approve commercial
deals belongs in separate governance and licensing-authority arrangements.
If both signatures are required on a customer agreement, that agreement
must make them a condition of effectiveness. The public licence does not
itself create those arrangements or the customer contract.

Customer agreements should specify the customer, covered software and
versions, authorised uses, fees, payment timing and duration. They cover
only rights their grantors may license and do not change other recipients'
research permissions. Contributions under the research licence alone do
not automatically grant the maintainers commercial relicensing rights;
broader contributor authorisation must be obtained separately where needed.

REVIEW BEFORE MERGING

- Resolve the infrastructure decision.
- Review the licence's scope, assent, remedies and liability provisions
  with appropriate legal counsel. No governing law or exclusive court is
  selected in the public licence.
- Retain records supporting the assumed licensing authority and establish
  the intended governance and commercial-contract arrangements.
- Preserve separately licensed material and notices. NOTICE identifies
  the retained MIT notice, existing Apache-2.0 source headers and dataset
  notices. This change is not a complete dependency or rights audit.
- Identify the first release containing the change when preparing a
  release; this PR does not choose a new package version.
